## 4. How to Log In

1. Open the SmartHorizon Web App at **[http://localhost:5173/](http://localhost:5173/)**
2. Click **Sign In / Portal Access** (or navigate to `/login`).
3. Enter any Email and Password from the tables above.
4. Redirection occurs automatically based on role:
   - Full Admins $\rightarrow$ `/dashboard`
   - Check-In Admins $\rightarrow$ `/checkin`
   - Data Entry Operators $\rightarrow$ `/admin/data-entry`
